    Frequently asked questions

    What if I don’t have my declarations page?
    Check your insurance company’s website or app, or the packet mailed at your last renewal — the declarations page is usually the first page or two. A clear phone photo of the paper copy works. You can also send the form without a file and we’ll help you track it down.
    Do I have to switch insurance?
    No. The review is free and there is no obligation. If your current policy already fits, we’ll tell you so. If we find options worth considering, you decide what happens next.
    How will you contact me?
    We’ll follow up by email or phone using the details you provide. If you prefer one over the other, tell us in the optional notes.
    Who sees my policy?
    Your policy is reviewed only by Bill or his licensed team. We don’t share, sell, or list your information. We’re a local North Carolina agency, not a lead-aggregator service. See our privacy policy.
