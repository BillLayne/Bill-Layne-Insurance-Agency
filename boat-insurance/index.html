BEFORE WE COMPARE

A better conversation starts with your details.

Your watercraft and how you use it help us discuss suitable options. A declarations page is a helpful starting point if you are already insured.

  • Hull year, make, model, length and identification number
  • Motor details, horsepower and trailer information
  • Storage address and waters where you operate
  • Operator experience, equipment and current policy

Boat insurance questions

Is my boat covered by homeowners insurance?

Homeowners coverage for boats can be limited. Have us review the boat, motor and policy before relying on that protection.

Why does the loss-settlement wording matter?

Different valuation terms can affect a covered payment. Compare the wording, values and deductibles rather than assuming every policy pays the same way.

Should I mention coastal or out-of-state use?

Yes. Tell us every area where you expect to boat so we can discuss navigation limits and eligibility.
