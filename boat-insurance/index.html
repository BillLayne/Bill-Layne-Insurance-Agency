COVERAGE QUESTIONS TO DISCUSS

Coverage that fits your plans.

A useful comparison starts with what you own, how you use it, and the protection you want to discuss.

Hull, motor and trailer

Review insured items, values, deductibles and how a covered loss is settled.

Liability on the water

Discuss liability limits and available medical payments coverage alongside the physical-damage protection.

Equipment and navigation

Ask about equipment, towing, navigation boundaries and any storage or seasonal restrictions.

Options, eligibility and coverage vary by carrier and policy. These are topics to review, not a guarantee that every item is covered.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you leave the dock.

Here are a few common questions we hear about boat insurance.

Can you review my current boat policy?

Yes. Share your declarations page and any changes to your watercraft, operators or use. Our team will review it with you.

What should I compare besides price?

Compare insured values, loss-settlement terms, liability limits, deductibles and exclusions. Tell us where you boat and how you store it.

What about towing, trailers and equipment?

Tell us what you want to protect. Our team will confirm available options, limits and restrictions with the carrier.
