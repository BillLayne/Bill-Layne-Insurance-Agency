# Content Pillars & Template Map

Six pillars keep the feed balanced. Rotate through them; never post the same
pillar twice in a row.

| # | Pillar | Templates | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Plain-English education** | `CarouselCoverSlide`+`CarouselTipSlide`+`CarouselCTASlide`, `QAPost`, `MythFactPost`, `TipsListPost` | The trust engine. One concept at a time, zero jargon. |
| 2 | **Local community** | `CommunityPost`, `PhotoPost` | Elkin, Dobson, Surry County events, schools, sponsorships. Out-reaches everything for a small-town page. |
| 3 | **Social proof** | `TestimonialPost`, `StarRating` | Genuine reviews only, first name + town. |
| 4 | **Seasonal & timely** | `ChecklistPost`, `StatPost`, `TipsListPost` | NC calendar: hurricane season (Jun–Nov), first frost, spring hail, holiday travel. |
| 5 | **Real humans / behind the scenes** | `PhotoPost` (Bill’s real photos), `HeroPromoPost` | The anti-AI play. Candid > polished. |
| 6 | **Offers & housekeeping** | `HeroPromoPost`, `GBPPost`, `HoursUpdatePost`, `CoverBanner` | Free quote reviews, holiday hours, announcements. At most ~1 in 5 posts is an ask. |

## The 12 proven post archetypes (2026)

1. **Myth vs. Fact** — `MythFactPost` (single) or a carousel; highest-save educational format.
2. **Coverage explainer carousel** — “What does liability actually pay for?” one idea per slide.
3. **Real review card** — `TestimonialPost`, 5 gold stars, “— Sarah W., Dobson”.
4. **Meet the team / real humans** — `PhotoPost` with a genuine portrait, name, years served.
5. **Big-number hook** — `StatPost`: “$64K” + “what an average dog-bite claim costs”.
6. **Seasonal NC checklist** — `ChecklistPost`: hurricane kit, winter pipes, hail season.
7. **FAQ answer** — `QAPost` with a real customer question; repeat keywords in caption.
8. **Life-event trigger** — `HeroPromoPost`: teen driver, new home, engagement ring, new truck.
9. **Local spotlight** — `CommunityPost`: fairs, football, farmers market.
10. **Behind-the-scenes lo-fi** — `PhotoPost`, candid, casual caption.
11. **“How we helped” story** — `QAPost` or `TestimonialPost` framing, anonymized and compliant.
12. **GBP offer/update** — `GBPPost`: free quote review, 58-char title, center-safe.

- “Your ______ isn’t covered the way you think”
- “We read your policy so you don’t have to: ______ edition”
