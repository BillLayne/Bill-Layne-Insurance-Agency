# 2026 Social Design Playbook — Bill Layne Insurance

How to make every post look current and perform. Researched July 2026.

## Format rules

- **Default every feed graphic to 4:5 portrait** (`SocialCanvas format="portrait"`, 1080×1350) on both Instagram AND Facebook. Meta officially recommends 4:5; portrait fills ~⅓ more mobile screen and earns ~23% more likes than square. 98% of Facebook users are on mobile.
- **Design for the grid crop.** Instagram crops profile-grid thumbnails to 3:4 — keep logos, headlines, and faces away from the edges. Turn on `safeGuides` while composing; turn it OFF before export.
- **Carousels are the engagement king** (roughly 10% avg engagement vs 7% single-image vs 6% Reels). Build education as an 8–12 slide carousel: `CarouselCoverSlide` → `CarouselTipSlide` × N → `CarouselCTASlide`, all on the same portrait canvas.
- **Micro-learning rule: 15–20 words max per slide**, one idea per slide, one visual anchor (the big outlined number, an icon, a stat).
- **The hook opens a curiosity gap, not a label.** “The one gap in your home policy nobody mentions” beats “Home insurance tips.”

## Type & look

- **One oversized, confident statement per post** — readable at thumbnail size (~150px wide). `Headline` size `xl`/`lg`, under ~8 words; the caption carries the detail.
- **Hand-marked emphasis** on exactly one phrase: `Highlight` (`marker`, `underline`, or `circle`). It’s the human, annotated-by-a-person accent that defines 2026 type.
- **Scale contrast** sells: huge number + small context (`StatPost`), giant slide number + short tip (`CarouselTipSlide`).
- **Texture beats sterile.** Canvas grain is on by default — “imperfect by design” reads human; flat AI-gradient sheen reads machine. Keep it on.
- Keep text overlay moderate on Facebook (~under 20% of the image area is still the safe habit) and image files under 1MB.

## Authenticity (the biggest 2026 trend)

- **54% of Americans report AI fatigue; ~52% of consumers disengage when content feels AI-generated.** “Made by humans” is now a selling point.
- **Real photos of real people beat stock and beat AI art.** Use Bill’s genuine photos, the actual office, real Elkin / Surry County scenery via `PhotoPost` + `SocialCanvas background="photo"`. Never use AI-generated people.
- Lo-fi candid content is a trust strategy, not a downgrade. Behind-the-scenes and community posts out-perform polish for local businesses.

## Reach mechanics

- **Saves and shares are the currency** (one share ≈ 50 likes to the algorithm). Checklists (`ChecklistPost`) get saved; local pride (`CommunityPost`) gets shared.
- **Hashtags are declining** — 3–5 relevant max. Instead front-load keywords in the caption’s first sentence (“Elkin NC auto insurance…”) — Instagram search reads captions semantically now.
- Facebook link posts get the least reach — post the graphic, put the link in the first comment.
- **Cadence:** Facebook 3–5×/week · Instagram 3–5×/week + ~2 Stories/day · GBP 1–2×/week. Consistency beats volume ~5×.
