GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Auto + renters, explained.

Can I bundle auto and renters insurance in North Carolina?

Depending on the carrier and your eligibility, you may qualify for a multi-policy discount. We can compare eligible bundle options and separate policies. A bundle is not automatically the lowest total price or the best coverage fit.

Do I need renters insurance if my landlord has insurance?

Your landlord’s policy generally protects the building, not your personal belongings or your personal liability. Renters insurance addresses different needs. Bring any insurance requirements from your lease so we can review them with you.

Will one renters policy cover my roommates?

Do not assume a roommate is covered by your policy. Tell us who lives in the home and how you are related. We can review who qualifies as an insured and whether separate policies are appropriate.

Can you help if I am moving to Charlotte, Raleigh or another NC city?

Yes. Our agency is based in Elkin and serves North Carolina residents statewide. Share your move date, new rental address and where you will normally keep your car so we can discuss the policies and effective dates you need.

Is renters insurance the same as rental car insurance?

No. Renters insurance is for people renting a home, apartment or townhome. Rental car reimbursement is an optional auto coverage with its own conditions. Tell us if you need either or both.

What should I prepare for an auto and renters quote?

Have your driver and vehicle information, rental address and unit number, desired start date, current policy details and any lease insurance requirements available. Consider the cost to replace your belongings and the deductibles you would be comfortable paying.

Can I request renters insurance without switching my auto policy?

Yes. Ask our team about renters-only options. We can also discuss whether comparing your auto policy makes sense; availability and eligibility depend on the insurer.
