A few helpful answers.

Is this a paid membership?

No. Client Advantage is a collection of tools and agency support, not a separate paid membership or insurance policy. There is no separate toolkit membership fee during the pilot.

Can every client sign in today?

Access is currently limited to invited pilot participants. Being a policyholder does not automatically activate toolkit access. Call (336) 835-1993 if you would like help or want to ask about access.

Will the toolkit store my home records?

The Home Improvement Log and Emergency Contact Packet hold your entries only in the current browser tab. Download a backup or print your records before closing it. This is not a cloud document vault; linked tools have their own saving options.

Does using a tool update my insurance?

No. An inventory, property lookup, home log or appointment request does not change your coverage or confirm a claim payment. Contact our team to discuss policy changes. Review AI suggestions and verify public property records before relying on them.
