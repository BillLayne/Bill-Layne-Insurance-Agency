The mutual difference

Owned by policyholders, not stockholders

NC Grange Mutual has been insuring North Carolina since 1928, and it is still owned by its policyholders. That single structural difference changes who the company answers to. There are no outside shareholders taking a cut — the people insured by the company are the people who own it.

  • North Carolina companyDecisions get made by people who live with the same weather, the same roads, and the same risks you do.
  • Local claims serviceYou are not routed through a national call center that has never seen a Yadkin Valley storm.
  • An independent agent in the middleWe place you with NC Grange when it is genuinely the better fit — and tell you when it is not.
Homeowners coverage

Protection built around your house, not a template

A homeowners policy is really several coverages working together. Here is what the core of an NC Grange home policy handles — your declarations page and policy forms control the actual limits and terms.

  • Dwelling & other structuresThe house itself, plus detached garages, sheds, and fences.
  • Personal propertyYour belongings — furniture, clothes, tools, electronics.
  • Personal liabilityIf someone is hurt on your property or you damage someone else's.
  • Loss of useSomewhere to stay when a covered loss makes the house unlivable.
