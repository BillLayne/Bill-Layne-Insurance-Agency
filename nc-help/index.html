Prescription costs

Find medication assistance and compare prices. Each program has its own rules.

Medications

NC MedAssist

Free prescription medications shipped to eligible North Carolina residents.

Who it’s for: Uninsured NC residents with qualifying household income (up to 300% of the federal poverty level).

Service area: North Carolina statewide

Check eligibility(866) 331-1348
More details

A nonprofit pharmacy that ships prescription medications to your door completely free . For NC residents with no insurance and household income under 300% of the poverty level. Enrollment lasts a year and there's no fee to apply.

Who it's for: Uninsured NC residents — especially anyone managing diabetes, heart disease, asthma, or other chronic conditions.

Medications

Cost Plus Drugs

Compare prescription prices at an online pharmacy. Medication, pharmacy labor, and shipping have costs.

Who it’s for: A valid prescription is needed. Check available medicines and insurance options.

Service area: Online pharmacy; medicine availability varies

Compare medication prices
More details

Cost Plus Drugs lists medicine prices with a 15% markup, a $5 pharmacy labor charge, and shipping added at checkout. Compare the full delivered price before ordering.

A prescription is needed. Search their current medication catalog and confirm whether your insurance is accepted.

Medications

GoodRx

Compare local pharmacy prices and find prescription discount coupons.

Who it’s for: People with or without insurance; ask your pharmacist to compare the coupon with your copay.

Service area: Participating pharmacies; compare your ZIP code

Find a coupon
More details

Free coupons that often beat your insurance copay at local pharmacies — including the ones right here in Elkin, Dobson, and Mount Airy. Compare prices at every pharmacy near you, show the coupon on your phone at pickup.

Pro tip: Check GoodRx even if you have insurance — the coupon price is sometimes lower than your copay. Just ask the pharmacist to compare.

Medications

NeedyMeds

Find manufacturer assistance programs for costly prescriptions.

Who it’s for: Eligibility depends on the medicine, income, insurance, and manufacturer program.

Service area: National directory; program-specific eligibility

Find medication help(800) 503-6897
More details

A nonprofit database of manufacturer patient assistance programs — the programs where drug makers give brand-name medications free or nearly free to families who qualify. If you take an expensive brand-name drug, look it up here first.

Who it's for: Anyone on a costly brand-name medication, insured or not. Most people have no idea these programs exist.

Medications

Insulin cost assistance

Medicare-covered insulin costs no more than $35 per product for a one-month supply. Other assistance has its own rules.

Who it’s for: Medicare beneficiaries and people who may qualify for manufacturer assistance.

Service area: Medicare or manufacturer program rules

Look up your program
More details

For each Part B- or Part D-covered insulin product, Medicare limits the cost of a one-month supply to no more than $35, with no insulin deductible. Manufacturer programs for people outside Medicare have separate eligibility and terms.

How to start: Search for your insulin brand’s savings program or ask your pharmacist which program applies to your prescription.

Medications

Search manufacturer patient support and direct purchase programs through America’s Medicines.

Service area: National directory; program-specific eligibility

Search assistance programs
More details

America’s Medicines is the pharmaceutical industry’s search tool for manufacturer patient support and direct purchase programs. Search by medication, then confirm eligibility and costs with the manufacturer.

Who it’s for: Patients and caregivers looking for another place to check after NeedyMeds for brand-name medication help.

Power & heating bills

Explore heating and utility assistance. Your county DSS is a starting point for LIEAP and CIP.

Utility bills

LIEAP — Winter Heating Help

A one-time payment to a heating provider for eligible households.

Who it’s for: Income and heating-responsibility rules apply. Priority households apply in December; other households January 1–March 31, while funds last.

Service area: North Carolina statewide; apply through county DSS

Apply at ePASSProgram details
More details

LIEAP provides a one-time payment to a heating provider. Households including a person age 60 or older, or a qualifying disabled person receiving NC Division of Aging and Adult Services services, may apply December 1–31. Other households may apply January 1–March 31, or until funds run out.

How to apply: Online at ePASS, or through your county DSS by phone or in person. Surry DSS: (336) 401-8700.

Utility bills

Crisis Intervention Program (CIP)

Help for a health-related or life-threatening heating or cooling crisis.

Who it’s for: Income, resources, and crisis requirements apply. Contact county DSS before service is disconnected.

Service area: North Carolina statewide; apply through county DSS

Check program eligibility
More details

CIP helps eligible households facing a heating or cooling crisis that threatens health or life. Applications run July 1–June 30 while funds remain. The standard benefit limit is up to $600 per state fiscal year; limits may increase when additional funds are available. County DSS must confirm the current limit and your eligibility.

Who it’s for: Households meeting the income test (150% of the federal poverty level), resource rules, and crisis requirements. A past-due bill or final notice does not by itself guarantee eligibility.

Utility bills

Duke Energy Share the Light Fund

Duke Energy customers can find local partners offering help with energy bills.

Who it’s for: Duke Energy customers; assistance depends on local partner rules and available funds.

Service area: Duke Energy service areas; local partner rules

Find payment assistance
More details

Duke Energy customers can get help with bills, deposits, and reconnection fees through more than 100 partner agencies across NC — over $1 million already distributed in 2026. Use their Payment Assistance Finder to see which local agency handles your area.

Bonus: Their Operation Fan Heat Relief gives free fans to qualifying seniors and adults with disabilities each summer — ask your local Area Agency on Aging.

Utility bills

Tri-County Christian Crisis Ministry

Local help with utility bills, medication, and clothing vouchers.

Who it’s for: Families in Elkin, Jonesville, Dobson, State Road, and Ronda. Call to confirm service area and availability.

Service area: Elkin, Jonesville, Dobson, State Road, and Ronda

View program details(336) 526-1089
More details

Right on North Bridge Street in Elkin — a few blocks from our office. They help with electric, water, and heating bills, plus medication and clothing vouchers, for families in the Elkin, Jonesville, Dobson, State Road, and Ronda areas.

Hours: Mon 1–4pm, Wed & Fri 9am–12pm. They serve the first 20 clients each day, so arrive early — and at least 30 minutes before closing.

Find nearby food resources and information about grocery assistance.

Food

Second Harvest Food Finder

Find nearby food pantries, soup kitchens, and meal programs by ZIP code.

Who it’s for: Northwest NC communities, including Surry, Yadkin, and Wilkes. Confirm requirements and hours with the pantry.

Service area: Northwest North Carolina; check your ZIP code

Search by ZIP code(336) 784-5770
More details

Second Harvest Food Bank of Northwest NC serves 18 counties including Surry, Yadkin, and Wilkes. Type your zip code into their Food Finder and see every partner pantry, soup kitchen, and meal program near you. Call the pantry first to confirm hours.

Who it's for: Anyone. Pantries don't ask for much — usually just where you live.

Food

SNAP / Food & Nutrition Services

Monthly grocery assistance through North Carolina’s EBT program.

Who it’s for: Eligible NC households; income and other program rules apply. Apply through ePASS or county DSS.

Service area: North Carolina statewide

Apply at ePASS
More details

North Carolina's food assistance (what most folks call food stamps) loads money onto an EBT card monthly for groceries. Many working families qualify and don't realize it — especially after hours get cut or a job change. Apply online in about 30 minutes.

How to apply: Online at ePASS, or in person at your county DSS office.

Food

FindHelp.org & NC 211

Search a wider range of local resources, including food, rent, childcare, and transportation.

Who it’s for: Anyone looking for a starting point; each listed service has its own rules.

Service area: NC 211 statewide; FindHelp by ZIP code

Search by ZIP codeDial 2-1-1
More details

Two big directories that cover everything — food, rent, childcare, transportation, counseling. FindHelp searches by zip code; NC 211 is a free 24/7 phone line (dial 2-1-1) with real people who speak English and Spanish.

Who it's for: When your need doesn't fit any box on this page, start here.

Find care, enrollment help, and unbiased Medicare counseling.

Health care

Surry Medical Ministries

Primary care and related services for eligible uninsured neighbors and NC Medicaid patients. Fees may apply.

Who it’s for: Uninsured residents in the clinic’s eligible service area, or NC Medicaid patients. Call to confirm enrollment, appointments, and costs.

Service area: Greater Surry County area; eligible ZIP codes

Call the clinicView clinic details
More details

The clinic offers primary care and related services. Call for an appointment and to confirm available services. Its eligibility page includes uninsured patients with qualifying income and ZIP codes, and NC Medicaid patients; coverage and fees depend on the patient’s circumstances.

Fees may apply for visits, medicines, or lab work. The clinic states that no one is denied services because of inability to pay. Ask the clinic about costs and financial assistance before your visit.

Clinic location: 951 Rockford Street, Mount Airy, NC 27030.

Clinic source pages checked October 12, 2026: clinic location and eligibility and fees.

Health care

SHIIP — Free Medicare Counseling

Free, unbiased Medicare counseling through North Carolina’s SHIIP program.

Who it’s for: People turning 65, on Medicare, or helping a family member understand Medicare.

Service area: North Carolina statewide

Find Medicare counseling(855) 408-1212
More details

North Carolina's Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program gives free, unbiased one-on-one Medicare help — when to enroll, comparing drug plans, reading statements. Trained counselors in every NC county, and they sell nothing.

Who it's for: Anyone turning 65, on Medicare, or helping a parent sort through plan choices. (Yes — we're an insurance agency telling you about a free alternative. It's genuinely good.)

Health care

NC Navigator Consortium

Free, one-on-one help understanding marketplace coverage and enrolling.

Who it’s for: People exploring HealthCare.gov coverage; English and Spanish assistance is available.

Service area: North Carolina marketplace enrollment assistance

Find enrollment help(855) 733-3711
More details

Federally trained navigators who help you shop HealthCare.gov plans, figure out financial assistance, and enroll — free, unbiased, one-on-one, in English or Spanish. Led by Legal Aid of North Carolina.

Who it's for: Anyone without job-based coverage. Many families are shocked how low the subsidized premiums can be.

Health care

NC Medicaid

Explore NC Medicaid and apply for health coverage at any time of year.

Who it’s for: Eligible NC residents; household income and other requirements apply.

Service area: North Carolina statewide

Apply at ePASS
More details

Since NC expanded Medicaid, hundreds of thousands of working adults who never qualified before now do — even without kids at home. There's no enrollment season; you can apply any day of the year .

How to apply: Online at ePASS, at your county DSS, or ask an NC Navigator (above) to walk you through it.

Internet

Affordable internet

The federal ACP discount ended in 2024. These options have separate eligibility and costs.

Internet

Spectrum Internet Assist

50 Mbps internet for qualifying households. The provider lists $25/month, with a $15/month option for certain qualifying assistance programs.

Who it’s for: Qualifying NSLP, Community Eligibility Provision, or SSI participants; documentation and other rules apply.

Service area: Provider service area; check your address

Check eligibility
More details

Spectrum lists Internet Assist with 50 Mbps service for $25/month. Certain qualifying NSLP, CEP, or SSI households may receive the $15/month rate. Advanced WiFi costs extra; confirm the full price and current terms before enrolling.

Internet

AT&T Access

Low-cost home internet. Price and speed depend on services available at your address.

Who it’s for: Qualifying government assistance or household income below 200% of the federal poverty guideline.

Service area: Provider service area; check your address

Check your address
More details

AT&T Access provides low-cost home internet to eligible households. The monthly price and speed depend on the service available at your address; use the provider’s address checker for current pricing.

Who it’s for: Households participating in a qualifying government assistance program, or with income below 200% of the federal poverty guideline. Documentation may be required.

Internet

Lifeline (FCC)

A federal benefit of up to $9.25/month toward qualifying phone or internet service.

Who it’s for: Income-qualified households or participants in eligible programs such as SNAP, Medicaid, or SSI. One benefit per household.

Service area: Participating providers; check availability

Check eligibility
More details

Lifeline can reduce qualifying phone or internet service costs by up to $9.25/month. Confirm that your chosen provider participates and whether its plan can be combined with other assistance.

Who it’s for: Income-qualified households or participants in eligible programs such as SNAP, Medicaid, or SSI. Only one Lifeline benefit is allowed per household.

Unclaimed money

Forgotten refunds, deposits, paychecks, and other property may be held by the NC Treasurer.

Unclaimed money

NCCash — NC Unclaimed Property

Search the official NC unclaimed property database. Searching and filing a claim are free.

Who it’s for: Anyone can search for themselves, family members, or a business.

Service area: North Carolina unclaimed property

Search NCCash(866) 622-2741
More details

Type your name (try maiden names and misspellings too) and see instantly if the state is holding money for you. Claims are free to file and typically process in about 90 days. Their NCCash Match program even mails checks under $5,000 automatically to verified owners.

Who it's for: Everyone. Search yourself, your kids, your parents, even a business you owned.

Unclaimed money

Never Pay a "Finder"

You can search and claim directly through NCCash without paying a finder.

Who it’s for: Anyone checking for unclaimed money. Use the official site or call center.

Service area: North Carolina unclaimed property

View official guidance
More details

Searching and claiming through NCCash.gov is free. You can contact its official call center for help, without hiring a paid finder.

Common questions

Is every program free?

This page includes free and low-cost help. Some programs provide free services to eligible households; internet plans and pharmacies may charge. Confirm fees, eligibility, and availability with the provider before applying.

How do I check eligibility?

Use the program’s official link or phone number. Ask about income, insurance, residency, documentation, costs, and available funding. The provider makes the eligibility decision.

Will applying affect my credit or insurance?

Check directly with the program about any credit check or information-sharing requirements. You do not need to purchase insurance from our agency to use this directory. We cannot guarantee how every provider uses application information.

What if I earn too much for income-based assistance?

NCCash searches, prescription price comparisons through GoodRx and Cost Plus Drugs, and SHIIP Medicare counseling are starting points that do not all use an income test. Check the details and any costs directly with each program.

Do you get paid if I use these links?

No. There are no affiliate links or referral fees on this page. We built it as a community resource. We are not affiliated with these assistance programs.

What if I need different help?

NC 211 and FindHelp can connect you with resources for rent, childcare, transportation, counseling, and other needs. Call 2-1-1 or search FindHelp by ZIP code. Our Elkin office can also try to point you in the right direction.

How do I check for unclaimed money in North Carolina?

Search your name at NCCash.gov, the NC Treasurer’s official unclaimed property site, or call 866-622-2741. Searching and filing a claim are free.

Where can I get help with a heating bill?

Explore LIEAP, the Crisis Intervention Program, and your county Department of Social Services. LIEAP has seasonal application dates, and CIP requires a qualifying heating or cooling crisis. Duke Energy customers can also check the Share the Light Fund.
