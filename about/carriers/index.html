Our Carrier Appointments in North Carolina

An independent agency only works if it has real choices. These are the carriers Bill Layne Insurance Agency is appointed with in North Carolina, and when each one tends to be the right fit. Each company also has its own guide under insurance companies we represent in NC. Reviewed by Bill Layne, September 2, 2026.

Home › About Bill Layne › Carrier Appointments

How we choose between them

Every carrier files its own rates and underwriting rules with the North Carolina Department of Insurance, so the same house or the same driver can be priced very differently from one company to the next. When you ask for a quote we run the risk through the companies below that have an appetite for it, compare coverage as well as price, and tell you which one we would pick and why. We are paid a commission by whichever carrier issues the policy; see our Advertising Disclosure.

Home insurance

NC Grange Mutual

Homeowners · Dwelling fire · Farm

A North Carolina mutual company, owned by its policyholders and writing only in this state. NC Grange underwrites each home individually rather than by checklist, which is why it is often our answer for older homes, homes with a trampoline, a dog, or an outbuilding that a national carrier declined, and rural properties. It offers the equipment breakdown and inland flood endorsements we explain in our NC Grange guide.

Read our NC Grange Mutual review and guide →

Alamance Farmers Mutual

Homeowners · Dwelling fire · Rural property

Another North Carolina mutual with deep roots in the Piedmont. We use Alamance Farmers for well-kept older homes, rural and farm-adjacent dwellings, and homeowners who want a company whose underwriters know the county they live in.

Read our Alamance Farmers Mutual North Carolina guide →

Nationwide

Homeowners · Auto · Bundles

Nationwide is our most common bundle carrier: one company for the house and the cars, with a multi-policy discount and one claims number. It fits newer and well-maintained homes and drivers with clean records who want a national brand with local agency service.

Read our Nationwide North Carolina guide →

Travelers

Homeowners · Auto · Umbrella

Travelers competes hard on preferred homes and preferred drivers, and it is often where we place higher-value homes, families who need an umbrella policy on top of home and auto, and customers who value a large national claims operation.

Read our Travelers North Carolina guide →

Foremost

Mobile & manufactured homes · Vacant & seasonal · Specialty property

Foremost is our specialist for mobile and manufactured homes, vacant or seasonal dwellings, and properties that fall outside a standard homeowners program. When a standard carrier says no, Foremost is usually the next call.

Read our Foremost North Carolina guide →

Auto insurance

Progressive

Auto · Motorcycle · DL-123 filings · Non-standard

Progressive writes the widest range of drivers we see, from clean records to drivers who need a North Carolina DL-123 filing after a lapse or suspension. Same-day filings, usage-based discounts, and flexible payment plans make it our go-to when a driver has points, a lapse, or a tight budget.

Read our Progressive North Carolina guide →

National General

Auto · Non-standard · DL-123 filings

National General (part of Allstate) is a strong second market for drivers with violations, accidents, or prior lapses, and for households that need a non-owner policy or a DL-123 certificate. We compare it against Progressive on nearly every non-standard quote.

Read our National General North Carolina guide →

Dairyland

Motorcycle · ATV · Non-standard auto

Dairyland is our motorcycle and off-road specialist and a third market for hard-to-place auto risks. It is often the answer for a rider who wants agreed-value or accessory coverage a standard auto carrier will not offer.

Read our Dairyland North Carolina guide →

Hagerty

Classic & collector cars

Hagerty insures classic, antique, and collector vehicles on an agreed-value basis with limited-use pricing. If you have a car that is worth more than the book says, this is where it belongs, not on a standard auto policy that would pay actual cash value.

Read our Hagerty classic car guide →

What "9+ carriers" means on this site

Our home and auto pages mention comparing "9+ carriers." That count is the appointments listed on this page; it changes as we add or drop a company, and not every carrier quotes every risk. Appointments are current as of the review date above. To confirm which company your policy is with, check your declarations page or ask us.
