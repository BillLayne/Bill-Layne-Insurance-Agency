Ask the team a question →
How do you choose which companies to quote?+

We start with your property, vehicles, household drivers, and coverage priorities. Then we review the companies with programs that may fit, comparing coverage, deductibles, and price. Availability and eligibility vary by company and policy.

Can I combine home and auto insurance?+

Several of our carriers offer both home and auto options. We can compare a bundle with separate policies to help you decide which available option fits your needs. Multi-policy discounts depend on carrier guidelines and eligibility.

Will every company offer me a quote?+

No. Each company has its own underwriting guidelines, and not every company offers every type of coverage. Our team helps identify the available options for your situation; a quote request does not guarantee eligibility or bind coverage.

How many insurance companies do you represent?+

We represent 10 core personal-lines carriers, plus Steadily as a specialty landlord market. We have eight primary home carriers and six standard auto carriers; some companies appear in both groups, so those totals are not added together. Hagerty is a separate classic and collector vehicle market. Foremost is a Farmers company, not an additional appointment. NCJUA, NCIUA, and the NC Reinsurance Facility are separate residual mechanisms and are not included in our standard carrier counts.

What is the NCJUA FAIR Plan?+

The North Carolina Joint Underwriting Association (NCJUA) provides a residual market for essential property insurance when standard-market coverage is unavailable. It is separate from our appointed carriers. Eligibility, location, property condition, and available coverage must be reviewed; applying does not guarantee acceptance or bind coverage.

Can you help with an existing policy?+

Yes. Our Service Center has payment, policy, and document resources. You can also call or text our team for help identifying your insurance company or finding the right next step.
