Who you're working with

Bill Layne has spent more than 20 years helping North Carolina families protect their cars, homes, and businesses. He founded Bill Layne Insurance Agency in Elkin in 2005 as an independent agency — meaning the agency works for you, not for one insurance company, and shops multiple carriers against each other on every quote.

The agency holds a 4.9/5 Google rating with 127+ reviews, is a member of the Independent Insurance Agents of North Carolina (IIANC), and serves clients in English and Spanish across two offices in Surry County.

Most of the homes and cars Bill insures are in Surry, Yadkin, Wilkes, and Stokes counties: brick ranches and farmhouses on wells and county water, older houses in Elkin and Mount Airy that national carriers hesitate over, pickups and commuter cars on US-21 and I-77, and the occasional classic car or motorcycle. That local book is why the guides on this site talk about fire protection classes, consent-to-rate notices, roof age, and DL-123 filings instead of generic advice.

How Bill works a quote

  • One set of details, every carrier with an appetite. You send the basics once. Bill runs the home or the cars through each of the companies the agency represents that will write the risk, then compares the coverage line by line, not just the bottom number.
  • A recommendation, with the reason. You get the carrier Bill would choose and why, including the trade-offs, and the second-best option so you can see the gap.
  • Straight talk on the parts that cost money. Wind and hail deductibles, older roofs paid at actual cash value, consent-to-rate premiums, and lapse penalties are explained before you sign, not at claim time.
  • Service after the sale. Policy changes, ID cards, mortgagee updates, DMV filings, and claims guidance come from the same office that sold the policy. Call, text, or use the Client Hub.
