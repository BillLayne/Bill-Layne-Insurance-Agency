A local connection

The person behind
the agency.

Bill founded Bill Layne Insurance Agency in Elkin in 2005. Today, the independent agency serves all 100 North Carolina counties from offices in Elkin and Dobson.

His approach is simple: explain the options, compare the details, and stay available when questions come up. The team helps you understand coverage, limits, and deductibles before you choose.

With local experience in Surry, Yadkin, Wilkes, and Stokes counties, Bill’s guidance covers practical questions about older homes, roof age, fire protection, insurance points, and North Carolina filings.
