How does the NC 50/100/50 liability change affect my quote?

The North Carolina minimum liability increase to 50/100/50, effective July 2025, raised the baseline cost of insurance by mandating higher coverage limits, making discounts vital for maintaining affordability.

If you haven't looked at your policy declarations page lately, you might notice your limits look different. As of July 2025, North Carolina law increased the minimum coverage to:

  • $50,000 for bodily injury per person.
  • $100,000 for bodily injury per accident.
  • $50,000 for property damage.

Did you know? Before this change, the limits were much lower (30/60/25). While this provides better protection for everyone on the road, it naturally increased premiums across the state. This is exactly why re-quoting with Nationwide’s 2026 discount programs is crucial—it helps offset this mandatory state-wide increase.
