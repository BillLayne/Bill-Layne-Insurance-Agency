What in the World is MedPay?

In the insurance world of North Carolina, MedPay is optional, but honestly? It shouldn't be. It’s a coverage on your auto policy that pays for reasonable and necessary medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident.

Here is the kicker: It pays out regardless of who caused the accident. Whether you hit a patch of ice on the Blue Ridge Parkway or someone rear-ends you at a stoplight in Surry County—MedPay checks cut through the red tape.

Why It’s Better Than Your Health Insurance

Most people in the Triad say, "Bill, I have health insurance! I don't need this!"

WRONG. Here is why MedPay is the ultimate sidekick:

✓ It pays your Health Insurance Deductibles (Yes, really!).
✓ It covers Ambulance fees (which are shockingly expensive).
✓ It pays for Chiropractic visits to fix that whiplash.
✓ It covers Dental repairs resulting from the crash.
✓ NO DEDUCTIBLE on the MedPay coverage itself. First dollar coverage.
