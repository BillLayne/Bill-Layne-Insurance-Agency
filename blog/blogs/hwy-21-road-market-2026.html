What is the Highway 21 Road Market?

If you've lived in Surry County long, you already know the feeling: the last full weekend of July rolls around, and suddenly every front yard, church lot, and fairground along US 21 is stacked with treasures. That's the US Highway 21 Road Market — 100-plus miles of yard sales, sidewalk sales, arts and crafts, and home-cooked food running from Harmony in northern Iredell County all the way up the Blue Ridge to Wytheville, Virginia.

The event grew out of a group called Take a Break from the Interstate, launched in 2014 to bring travelers off I-77 and back onto the old two-lane road. US 21 was once the main "Lakes-to-Florida Highway," and the Road Market gives that historic route its busy weekend back — good for the towns, and a whole lot of fun for the rest of us.

📖 In plain English

Road market — a coordinated, multi-town yard sale strung along one highway. Nobody runs a single big market; hundreds of individual sellers, churches, and civic groups set up on the same weekend, and you shop your way down the road.

Mark July 24–26 — it's always the last full weekend of July, and the whole 100-mile route runs all three days.

Where does the route go, town by town?

The Road Market follows US 21 south to north. You can start from whichever end is closest — Harmony if you're coming from the Statesville side, or drop in at Elkin, which sits right in the middle. Here's the run of towns and communities, gold pins marking the biggest vendor hubs:

  • HarmonyIredell Co.

    The southern starting point. Sellers set up early to catch shoppers heading north up the route.

  • Hamptonville & Brooks CrossroadsYadkin Co.

    Rural stretches with roadside vendors, antiques, and handmade goods in Yadkin County.

  • JonesvilleYadkin Co.

    Vendors line the roadside just before the Yadkin River crossing into Elkin.

  • ElkinSurry Co. · Hub

    A major stop near the middle of the route. Check the current local event listing or Yadkin Valley Chamber guidance for vendor locations and registration details, then explore Main Street shops, wineries, and restaurants while you're here.

  • State Road & ThurmondWilkes–Surry line · Hub

    One of the biggest concentrations on the route — the Thurmond Community Center rents dozens of vendor spaces inside and out, plus food.

  • Roaring Gap & Cherry LaneAlleghany Co.

    The scenic climb up the mountain, with long-range Blue Ridge views opening up along the way.

  • SpartaAlleghany Co. · Hub

    A mountain town near 3,300 ft. Vendors gather at the Higgins Fairgrounds and along US 21 — a great turnaround or lunch stop.

  • Independence & Elk CreekGrayson Co., VA

    The route crosses into Virginia through scenic Grayson County with rural vendors and mountain scenery.

  • WythevilleWythe Co., VA

    The northern endpoint — a good overnight or turnaround point if you're shopping the full 100 miles.

Elkin sits at the middle of the route, so it's the easiest place to jump in if you only have a few hours.

A local tip

If it's your first year, don't try to do all 100 miles. Pick Elkin up to Sparta — you'll hit the biggest hubs, catch the mountain views, and still be home for supper.

How do I shop it like a local?

A little planning turns a long, hot drive into a great day. Here's how the regulars work the route:

  1. Start early and pick your end

    Serious shoppers hit the road at daybreak on Friday. Start from whichever end of U.S. 21 is closest to you and head toward the other.

  2. Map the big hubs

    Note the largest vendor clusters — downtown Elkin, the Thurmond Community Center, and the Higgins Fairgrounds in Sparta — so you don't drive past them.

  3. Bring cash and a plan

    Carry small bills for easy haggling, a tape measure for furniture, and blankets or straps to protect your finds on the drive home.

  4. Save Sunday for deals

    Vendors would rather sell than repack on the last day, so Sunday is the best time to negotiate on whatever is left.

Saturday is the busiest day, so plan extra time for traffic along the two-lane road. In Elkin, swing by the Farmers Market (9 a.m.–noon Saturday at Town Hall) and grab lunch downtown to cool off between stops.

Selling from your yard? Read this first.

Plenty of folks along US 21 set up in their own front yard for the weekend — and it's usually a safe, simple way to make a little money and clear out the garage. Just remember that once you invite the public onto your property, a stranger tripping on a garden hose or a curious kid reaching for a power tool becomes your concern.

The good news: your homeowners policy generally includes personal liability that can help if a shopper is accidentally injured on your property. It's not a guarantee — coverage depends on your policy — so it's worth a quick call to make sure your limits are where you want them before the crowds arrive.

📖 In plain English

Personal liability coverage — the part of your homeowners policy that can help pay if someone who isn't in your household is accidentally hurt on your property, or if you accidentally damage someone else's things.

A quick pre-sale safety pass

Before you set out the first table, walk the yard the way a shopper will: coil up hoses and extension cords, move step ladders and lawn mowers where they can't be started, put pets inside or on a leash, and keep the crowd out of the house. Small stuff — but it's what keeps a fun weekend from turning into a claim.

Your homeowners liability may help if a shopper is hurt in your yard — but clearing hazards first is what actually prevents the injury.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Highway 21 Road Market in 2026?
The 2026 U.S. Highway 21 Road Market runs Friday through Sunday, July 24–26. It's always held the last full weekend of July, and shopping is free — you just drive the route and stop wherever a sale catches your eye.
Where does the Highway 21 yard sale start and end?
The route stretches about 100 miles along Historic U.S. Highway 21, from Harmony in northern Iredell County, NC, up through Jonesville, Elkin, State Road and Sparta, and on to Wytheville, Virginia. You can start from whichever end is closest and head toward the other.
Do I need a permit to set up a yard sale on the route?
Rules vary by town, county, and property location. Check current requirements with the local municipality, event contact, or property owner before setting up, even though residents, churches, civic groups, and businesses participate along the corridor.
Does my homeowners insurance cover a yard sale?
Usually your homeowners liability helps if a shopper is accidentally injured on your property, but it isn't a guarantee — it depends on your policy. It's worth a quick call before the weekend to confirm your limits and to clear tripping hazards, tools, and pets out of the way.
What towns are on the Highway 21 Road Market route?
On the NC side the route passes through Harmony and Houstonville, Hamptonville, Brooks Crossroads and Jonesville, Elkin, State Road and Thurmond, and Roaring Gap, Cherry Lane and Sparta, then continues into Grayson and Wythe counties in Virginia toward Wytheville.
