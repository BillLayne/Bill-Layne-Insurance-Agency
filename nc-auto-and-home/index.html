Good questions. Straight answers.

Is bundling auto and home in North Carolina always less expensive?

No. While a multi-policy discount may be available with one carrier, pairing two separate carriers (for example, one carrier for auto and a mutual or regional carrier for homeowners) can sometimes offer a better combined price or coverage fit. We compare both bundled and separate options across all 100 NC counties. Rates, eligibility, and discounts vary by carrier and underwriting.

Can I compare just auto or just home right now?

Yes. You can start a dedicated North Carolina Auto Quote, start a North Carolina Home Quote (with our 60-second “Find My Home” property lookup), or request a combined Auto + Home comparison.

How does the 60-second “Find My Home” property lookup work?

On our Home Quote page, enter your North Carolina property address and tap Find My Home Details. Our tool pulls public county property records—including year built, heated square footage, stories, and exterior details—and lets you edit any item if you've updated your home.

Can I switch homeowners insurance if my mortgage pays through escrow?

Yes. Once you review and approve a new policy, our licensed team sends the new declarations page and invoice directly to your mortgage servicer's insurance department and helps you handle the cancellation and refund from your prior carrier. See our NC Escrow Switch Guide for details.
