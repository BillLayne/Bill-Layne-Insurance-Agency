Bundle FAQ

Common bundle questions

Quick answers. Call (336) 835-1993 if you don't see yours.

Many NC carriers offer eligible multi-policy discounts when you bundle auto and home. Actual savings depend on your driving record, home characteristics, claims history, coverage choices, and which carrier offers the best combined rate. As an independent agency, we compare bundled and split-policy options across our carriers.
Not always. Sometimes splitting policies between two carriers (one for auto, one for home) saves more than bundling with a single carrier, especially if you have an older home or a less-than-perfect driving record. We compare both options before recommending.
Bill Layne Insurance compares options across 6 auto carriers and 9+ home carrier and specialty markets, including Nationwide, Progressive, Travelers, National General, Foremost, Alamance Farmers Mutual, and NC Grange Mutual. Availability, eligibility, and multi-policy discounts vary by household and property.
Many carriers may offer eligible multi-policy discounts for auto with renters or condo insurance. Availability and savings vary by carrier, location, underwriting, coverage selections, and household details, so we compare the combined price instead of assuming a bundle is automatically cheapest.
You can add a second policy at any time. For the smoothest discount application, we recommend bundling at the renewal of your existing policy or at the start of a new one. Call us at (336) 835-1993 and we'll handle the timing.
For auto: driver license numbers, vehicle VINs (or year/make/model), and current coverage limits. For home: address, year built, square footage, roof age, and current dwelling coverage. We can also pull your existing declarations pages with your permission.
Filing a claim can affect your rate at renewal regardless of bundling. However, bundled policies through a single carrier are often re-rated together, so a claim on one policy could influence the renewal premium on the other. We'll review your full risk picture before recommending a structure.
We can often prepare bundle comparisons within 1 business day after receiving the needed declarations pages or quote information. Once you choose an option, our licensed team confirms underwriting and binding requirements and can help coordinate documents for your mortgage lender and prior carrier.
