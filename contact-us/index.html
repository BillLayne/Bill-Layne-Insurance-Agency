HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you get in touch.

A few common questions about quotes, service, and reaching our team.

Visit the Service Center →
What are your office hours?

Our offices are open Monday through Friday, 9am–5pm. Call our Elkin team at 336-835-1993 or our bilingual Dobson office at 336-356-2200.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Our team personally reviews your request and compares available carrier options. Timing depends on the coverage and details needed. Call us if you have a specific deadline; pricing is not instant.

Can I request a quote online?

Yes. Start with our online quote options, call 336-835-1993, or visit either office. Our local team will follow up with you.

What should I do if I have a claim after hours?

Call your insurance carrier’s claims line, listed on your insurance card or policy documents. Contact our office the next business day for help with the process.

Do you offer payment plans?

Payment options vary by carrier and policy. Ask our team to review the available payment plans with you.

Can you help with claims?

We can help you contact your carrier, understand the next steps, and follow up with questions during the claims process.
