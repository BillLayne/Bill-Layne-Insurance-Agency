Small detail, big difference

Tell the full story of how your household drives.

Leaving out a driver, commute, business use, or vehicle can make a quote look better than the final price. Clear details now help prevent surprises later.

If you are unsure whether something matters, ask. A five-minute question is easier than fixing a policy after a loss.

Step two

The big insurance words, made small.

You do not need to memorize these. You only need to know what each part is trying to protect.

01Liability+

Helps pay for injuries or property damage when you are legally responsible for a crash.

Ask: Are these limits enough to protect what I own?

02Uninsured / underinsured driver+

Helps when the driver who caused the crash has no insurance or not enough insurance.

Ask: What does this cover for people and for the car?

03Collision+

Helps repair or replace your car after a crash, minus your deductible.

Ask: Could I comfortably pay this deductible tomorrow?

04Comprehensive+

Helps with non-crash damage such as theft, hail, flood, fire, glass, or hitting an animal.

Ask: Is glass handled differently on this policy?

05Medical payments+

Can help with accident-related medical or funeral costs, up to the policy limit.

Ask: Who in my household is covered?

06Rental and roadside help+

Optional help with a rental car during covered repairs or towing when your car is disabled.

Ask: What are the daily limits and service limits?

NC
A quick note for North Carolina drivers

North Carolina requires liability and uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage. For policies issued or renewed on or after July 1, 2025, the minimum liability limits are 50/100/50. A lender may also require collision and comprehensive coverage on a financed vehicle.

Quick answers

Auto insurance shopping FAQs

Four simple answers to the questions drivers ask most often.

What information do I need to shop for car insurance?+

Gather driver names, birth dates and license information; vehicle year, make, model and VIN; how each vehicle is used; your current declarations page; and details about licensed household members.

Should every quote use the same coverage?+

Yes. Compare the same liability limits, deductibles, optional coverages, drivers and vehicles before comparing price. Otherwise, a lower quote may simply provide less protection.

When should I cancel my old policy?+

Confirm that the new policy is active and that you have your new insurance ID cards before canceling the old policy. If the vehicle is financed, confirm the lender information is correct.

Is the cheapest quote always the best choice?+

Not always. Review limits, deductibles, optional benefits, payment fees and the full policy-term cost. The best choice balances an affordable price with protection you understand.
