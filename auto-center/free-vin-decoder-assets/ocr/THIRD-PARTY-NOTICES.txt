Self-hosted browser OCR components
==================================

This directory contains pinned, locally served copies of:

- Tesseract.js 5.1.1
  Source: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js/tree/v5.1.1
- tesseract.js-core 5.1.1
  Source: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core/tree/v5.1.1
- English trained data from @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0
  Source: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tesseract.js-data/eng/v/1.0.0

These components are licensed under the Apache License 2.0. A copy is in
LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt. They are loaded only after a visitor chooses to scan
a VIN label or license plate. OCR runs in a browser worker; the selected photo
is not uploaded to an OCR service.
