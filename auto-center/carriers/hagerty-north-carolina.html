Small details. A clearer picture.

Does my vehicle qualify?

Hagerty considers many classic, collector, modified vehicles and classic motorcycles. Eligibility depends on the vehicle, drivers, use and storage. Your collector car generally cannot be your daily driver. Hagerty makes the final eligibility decision.

How is the vehicle’s value determined?

You and Hagerty agree on an insured value using your vehicle’s condition, restoration details and supporting information. For a covered total loss, policy terms apply, including any deductible and any applicable retained-salvage deduction. Review the agreed value as your vehicle changes.

Can I drive my classic for pleasure?

Hagerty allows occasional pleasure driving, club events and shows for eligible vehicles. Daily commuting and routine errands are not the intended use. Mileage and storage requirements depend on the vehicle and underwriting review.

What information do I need for a quote?

Start with your vehicle’s year, make, model, your contact details and North Carolina ZIP code. We’ll also discuss estimated value, modifications, use and storage. Photos, driver information and details of your regular-use insurance may be needed to complete the quote.
