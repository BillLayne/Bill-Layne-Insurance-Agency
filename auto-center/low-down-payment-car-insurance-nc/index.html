The basics

What the down payment really is

The down payment is the first payment that starts your policy. It is not a separate fee and it is not extra coverage. The insurance company takes the premium for the whole policy term, usually six or twelve months, and splits it: one payment to start, then the rest in the bills that follow.

A low down payment usually means the rest of the premium is spread across more payments. It can make the first day easier on your budget. It does not, by itself, make the policy cheaper overall.

Before you choose

Ask for three numbers and one date

  1. Due today.The first payment that starts the policy.
  2. Each payment after that, and how many.Ask whether every payment includes an installment fee, and what happens if one is late.
  3. The total for the whole policy term.This is the number that tells you which plan actually costs less.

The date is the start date. If you are buying a car, the policy needs to start on the day you pick it up, because the NCDMV needs proof of insurance to register it.

Neither is right for everyone. Here is what changes.

Compare fairly

Compare the same coverage, not just the first payment

A smaller first payment can come from a smaller policy. Before you choose, make sure every quote has the same coverage.

  • Same liability limitsNorth Carolina requires at least 50/100/50. Many drivers carry more.
  • Same deductiblesIf the quote includes comprehensive and collision.
  • Same drivers and carsEveryone in the household who drives, listed the same way.
  • Same start date and termSix months and twelve months are not the same price.

Straight answers

Questions about down payments and payment plans

What is a down payment on car insurance?+

It is the first payment that starts your policy. The insurance company sets the amount, and it depends on the company, the payment plan you choose, your driving record, your car and where you live. The rest of the premium for the policy term is split into the payments that follow.

Is the first payment the same as the monthly bill?+

Not always. The first payment is often larger than the bills that follow, and some plans add a fee to each payment. Ask for the amount due today, each payment after that, how many payments there are, and the total for the whole policy term.

Does paying the down payment start my coverage?+

Coverage starts once the insurance company accepts your application and receives the first payment. The company then reports your coverage to the NCDMV electronically on a Certificate of Insurance, called an FS-1. Until then, a quote is only a price.

Can I get car insurance with no down payment?+

A policy starts when the company receives the first payment, so there is always something due to start it. How much that is depends on the company and the plan. We compare the companies we represent to find a first payment that fits your budget.

Does a low down payment mean less coverage?+

No. The payment plan and the coverage are separate choices. A lower first payment usually means the rest of the premium is spread over more payments. To compare fairly, quote the same limits, deductibles, drivers and cars.

What happens if I miss a payment?+

The company can cancel the policy for non-payment. If the car still has a North Carolina plate, that is a lapse, and the NCDMV can charge a civil penalty. If a payment is going to be late, call us before the cancellation date on your notice.
