Quick Answer

Buying a car in North Carolina adds a 3% highway use tax (at a dealer, on the price plus the doc fee, minus your trade-in), a $66.75 title fee and a $46.25 yearly registration, plus your county's vehicle property tax every year. Electric cars pay $214.50 more a year and plug-in hybrids $107.25 more. Insurance is the other big cost, and you need it before you can register the car.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How much is the highway use tax on a car in North Carolina?

      North Carolina charges a 3% highway use tax when a vehicle title is transferred. When you buy from a dealer, the tax is figured on the sales price plus the dealer’s doc or admin fee, minus any trade-in allowance (N.C.G.S. 105-187.3). The most you pay is $250 if the car has been titled in your name in another state for at least 90 days before you apply for an NC title (N.C.G.S. 105-187.6).

      What are the title and registration fees in North Carolina?

      The NCDMV fee schedule lists a certificate of title at $66.75 and a private passenger vehicle registration at $46.25. Electric vehicles pay an extra $214.50 and plug-in hybrids an extra $107.25 on top of registration. Durham, Orange and Wake counties add $17 in county and regional transportation taxes, and Randolph County adds $2.

      Do you pay property tax on a car in North Carolina?

      Yes. Counties tax registered vehicles every year. Under the Tag & Tax Together program, you pay the vehicle property tax at the same time as your registration renewal. A newly purchased vehicle can defer the property tax for 60 days with a limited registration plate, and the tax must then be paid within 60 days.

      Does a trade-in lower the highway use tax in NC?

      Yes. For a sale by a dealer, North Carolina figures the highway use tax on the sales price plus the dealer’s doc or admin fee, less the allowance the dealer gives for your trade-in (N.C.G.S. 105-187.3).

      Do I need car insurance before I register a car in North Carolina?

      Yes. North Carolina requires active liability insurance of at least 50/100/50 on every registered vehicle, and NCDMV checks for it when you register. If you finance or lease, your lender will usually require comprehensive and collision coverage too, so get your insurance price before you sign.
