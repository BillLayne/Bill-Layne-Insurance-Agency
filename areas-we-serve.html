A few local questions, answered.

Quick answers about our service area and getting started.

Can you help me anywhere in North Carolina?

Yes. Our agency serves North Carolina statewide. Our offices are in Elkin and Dobson, and you can work with our team by phone or online. Carrier eligibility and available coverage depend on your situation.

Do I need to visit an office?

No. You can start a quote request online or call our team. If you prefer to visit, our Elkin and Dobson offices are open Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm Eastern Time.

How do I find insurance options for my address?

Search for an existing community guide above, browse the county directory, or start a quote request. Share your address and coverage needs so our team can review available carrier options. We can help even if your town does not have a separate guide.

Can you help me compare multiple types of insurance?

Yes. We help with auto, home, mobile home, renters, landlord rental property and specialty insurance, including motorcycle, ATV, boat, PWC and collector car coverage. Our team reviews eligible options from the carriers we represent and explains the coverage, limits and deductibles.

More local coverage questions
What should I know about local weather and home coverage?

Ask us to review wind and hail deductibles, water exclusions and standard surface-water exclusions for your property. Coverage depends on your policy terms, limits and exclusions. Our home insurance guides explain the details to discuss with our team.

Do insurance prices vary by community?

Your address can affect available options and pricing, along with your property or vehicle details, coverage choices and other rating factors. A personalized comparison is more useful than a general county-to-county price claim.

How do I get help with a claim?

Visit our Claims Center for carrier reporting contacts and documentation guidance, or call our team for help finding your next step. The carrier handles claim decisions and payments under the policy.

Can I compare auto and home or renters together?

Yes. Ask us to compare available multi-policy options alongside separate policies. Eligibility and discounts vary by carrier; compare the coverage, deductibles and overall cost. Savings are never guaranteed.
